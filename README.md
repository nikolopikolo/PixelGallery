# PixelGallery

**A lightweight, open-source photo gallery for Wear OS.**

PixelGallery brings a simple and intuitive photo viewing experience to Wear OS smartwatches. Transfer images directly from your Android phone over local Wi-Fi, browse your gallery offline, and explore photos using intuitive touch gestures and digital crown controls.

Designed with simplicity and performance in mind, PixelGallery consists of two native Android applications: a phone companion for managing and transferring photographs, and a standalone Wear OS gallery for viewing them.

## Features

**Android Companion**
- Automatic smartwatch discovery over local Wi-Fi.
- PIN-based device pairing.
- Wireless photo transfers.
- Multiple image selection.
- Transfer progress tracking.
- Photo management.

**Wear OS Gallery**
- Offline photo storage and viewing.
- Scrollable photo gallery.
- Fullscreen image viewer.
- Pinch-to-zoom and double-tap zoom.
- Digital crown zoom controls.
- Swipe navigation between photographs.
- Optional keep-screen-awake functionality.

## Compatibility

Developed and tested using the Google Pixel Watch 3 (45mm) and Google Pixel 6 Pro.

Compatibility with other Android phones and Wear OS smartwatches may vary.
