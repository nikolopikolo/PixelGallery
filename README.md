# PixelGallery

**A lightweight, open-source photo gallery for Wear OS.**

PixelGallery brings a simple and intuitive photo viewing experience to Wear OS smartwatches. Transfer images directly from your Android phone over local Wi-Fi, browse your gallery offline, and explore photos using intuitive touch gestures and digital crown controls.

Designed with simplicity and performance in mind, PixelGallery consists of two native Android applications: a phone companion for managing and transferring photographs, and a standalone Wear OS gallery for viewing them.

## Features

### Android Companion
- Automatic smartwatch discovery over local Wi-Fi.
- PIN-based device pairing.
- Wireless photo transfers.
- Multiple image selection.
- Transfer progress tracking.
- Photo management.

### Wear OS Gallery
- Offline photo storage and viewing.
- Scrollable photo gallery.
- Fullscreen image viewer.
- Pinch-to-zoom and double-tap zoom.
- Digital crown zoom controls.
- Swipe navigation between photographs.
- Optional keep-screen-awake functionality.

## Installation

PixelGallery consists of **two separate APKs**, both of which are required for the complete experience.

Download both APKs from the latest GitHub release.

### 1. Android Phone Installation

Download the **Mobile APK**.

This APK is intended for your **Android smartphone**, not your smartwatch.

Install it on your phone like a regular Android application. You may need to allow installation from unknown sources in your Android settings.

The mobile application allows you to discover your smartwatch, pair with it, and transfer photographs.

### 2. Wear OS Installation

Download the **Wear APK**.

This APK is intended for your **Wear OS smartwatch**, not your smartphone.

Unlike the mobile application, the Wear OS APK generally needs to be **sideloaded** onto your smartwatch.

Several open-source projects available on GitHub provide tools for installing APK files on Wear OS devices.

Search for **"Wear OS APK sideload GitHub"** or **"how to install APK on Wear OS"** to find a suitable tool and installation guide.

Follow the instructions provided by your chosen sideloading tool.

**Note:** Installing an APK on a Wear OS smartwatch may require enabling Developer Options and wireless debugging.

### 3. Connecting Your Devices

Once both applications are installed:

1. Connect your Android phone and Wear OS smartwatch to the same local Wi-Fi network.
2. Open PixelGallery on both devices.
3. Use the mobile application to discover your smartwatch.
4. Follow the PIN-based pairing process.
5. Select photographs on your phone and transfer them to your watch.
6. Open the Wear OS gallery to browse your photographs.

Transferred photographs are stored locally on your smartwatch and can be viewed offline.

## Compatibility

Developed and tested using the Google Pixel Watch 3 (45mm) and Google Pixel 6 Pro.

Compatibility with other Android phones and Wear OS smartwatches may vary.
